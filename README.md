# Tiny GPT

Training a small GPT-style language model from scratch on a single laptop GPU (RTX 3060 Laptop, 6 GB VRAM).

## Goal

Build and train a working tiny language model end to end, and understand every part of it.

- **Timeline:** 4 weeks, October 12 – November 8, 2026
- **Schedule:** three 1.5-hour sessions a week (Monday, Friday, Sunday)

## How I'm building it

I'm a first-year AI and Data Engineering student at Yıldız Technical University. I'm building this in a learning mode with AI help (Claude): the model is written in small pieces, and I don't move on until I understand the piece in front of me.
